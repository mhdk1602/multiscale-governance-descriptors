## Why this is not a con

Hybrid subscription-based publication is the historical academic publishing model. Author publishes for free; subscribers pay. The recent shift toward "Gold OA" with author-paid APCs is a relatively recent business model change by Elsevier, Springer, ACM, and IEEE. For an independent researcher with no institutional APC budget, subscription-based publication remains a legitimate path. The paper is just as citable, just as peer-reviewed, just as indexed in databases (Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar). The only difference is that readers without institutional access have to find a copy (which the Zenodo preprint already provides).
